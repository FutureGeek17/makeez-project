Al Khawaneej & Za’abeel

Makeez proudly serves Dubai from two vibrant neighborhoods: Al Khawaneej and Za’abeel. Whether you're in the mood for a late-night snack or a bold comfort food fix, our kitchens are strategically placed to deliver quickly and reliably across key areas of the city.

We operate daily from 1 PM until the early hours, specializing in delivery-only service—no dine-in, no lines, just fast, flavorful food brought straight to your doorstep.

From gourmet burgers and loaded fries to fusion noodles and juicy steaks, Makeez is your go-to kitchen when cravings hit.

Wherever you are in Dubai, if you’re near Al Khawaneej or Za’abeel, you're just a few clicks away from bold, crave-worthy comfort food.
